Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy covers the "Gospel at Dawn Upload" application, a private tool used only by the owner of the Gospel at Dawn YouTube channel (youtube.com/@gospelatdawn).

What the app does

The app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, with the channel owner's own Google account, to upload and schedule videos, manage playlists, captions and thumbnails, read and reply to comments on the channel, and read the channel's statistics.

Information we access

The app only accesses data of the Gospel at Dawn channel: its videos, playlists, comments and statistics. Public comments left by viewers on the channel are read so the channel can reply to them. The app does not sign in any other user and does not collect personal information from viewers.

How information is used and stored

Information is used only to run the channel. It is stored on the channel owner's own computer and is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties, and it is not used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is handled under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
